Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026
Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Your Privacy Matters: Sentinel is designed to assist authorized Discord moderators by identifying potential rule violations. Message monitoring and AI analysis are performed for legitimate community moderation purposes.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sentinel Moderation ("Sentinel", "we", "our", or "us") collects, processes, uses, and protects information obtained through the Sentinel Discord application.

Sentinel is an AI-powered moderation assistant intended to help authorized server staff identify potential rule violations and concerning conversations.

This policy applies to information processed by Sentinel in Discord servers where the application has been authorized to operate.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and the permissions granted, Sentinel may process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames.
  • Discord server and channel identifiers.
  • Message content from accessible channels.
  • Message timestamps and message identifiers.
  • Relevant conversation history and context.
  • Links to Discord messages.
  • Bot configuration and monitoring preferences.
  • Moderation alerts and associated analysis results.

Sentinel may process messages from multiple participants within a conversation to help distinguish potential rule violations from ordinary discussion.

Sentinel does not require Discord account passwords, payment card details, or personal login credentials.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information is obtained through Discord's authorized application programming interfaces (APIs), subject to the permissions granted to Sentinel.

Collection may occur when:

  • New messages are posted in monitored channels.
  • Authorized staff request historical message analysis.
  • Administrators configure monitoring settings.
  • Sentinel generates moderation alerts.

Sentinel is not intended to access private messages between unrelated Discord users or channels for which the application lacks authorization.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

Sentinel processes information to provide moderation assistance, including:

  • Identifying potential violations of server rules.
  • Detecting harassment and discriminatory behavior.
  • Identifying spam and prohibited discussions.
  • Evaluating conversational context.
  • Generating private moderation reports.
  • Reducing duplicate alerts.
  • Maintaining application settings and functionality.
  • Supporting authorized moderation investigations.

Sentinel's assessments are advisory. Human moderators remain responsible for reviewing reports and determining appropriate actions.

5. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Sentinel may use third-party artificial intelligence services, including OpenAI's API, to analyze relevant messages and conversation context.

Information submitted for analysis may include message text, relevant conversational excerpts, and identifiers or metadata necessary to understand the discussion.

Third-party providers process submitted information according to their applicable service terms, privacy policies, and data processing arrangements.

Sentinel aims to limit submitted information to what is reasonably necessary for moderation.

AI-generated assessments may be inaccurate and should not be treated as conclusive evidence.

6. Moderation Reports and Disclosure

When Sentinel identifies a potential rule violation, it may generate a report containing:

  • Relevant message excerpts.
  • Discord user references.
  • Channel and message links.
  • Possible rule violations.
  • Severity or confidence assessments.
  • Contextual explanations.

Reports may be delivered through Discord direct messages to designated authorized moderators.

Report recipients are expected to use this information only for legitimate moderation purposes.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address security incidents or misuse of the service.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Sentinel may temporarily process message content in memory while evaluating conversations.

Application configuration, message identifiers, alert records, or other operational information may be stored where necessary to provide the service and prevent duplicate reports.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the deployed application configuration, and applicable technical or legal requirements.

Information should not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Specific retention periods and deletion procedures will be documented once the deployed configuration has been finalized.

8. Data Security

Sentinel's operator aims to implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information processed by the application.

These safeguards may include:

  • Restricting access to authorized operators.
  • Protecting bot tokens and API credentials.
  • Limiting Discord permissions where practical.
  • Restricting access to moderation reports.
  • Maintaining appropriate application configuration.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Access and Deletion Requests

Individuals may contact Sentinel Moderation to request information about personal data processed by the application or to request deletion of eligible information.

Requests will be evaluated according to applicable laws, Discord's platform requirements, and legitimate security or moderation needs.

Some information may need to be retained where legally required or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

To submit a privacy or deletion request, email: sentinelmoderation@gmail.com

10. Children's Privacy

Sentinel operates within Discord communities and is not intended to independently solicit personal information from children.

Sentinel does not require users to submit personal information directly to access its moderation functionality.

If you believe information has been processed inappropriately, please contact us using the information provided below.

11. Third-Party Services

Sentinel relies on third-party services to provide its functionality.

These may include:

  • Discord for communication and message access.
  • OpenAI for AI-powered analysis, when enabled.
  • Application hosting and infrastructure providers used to operate Sentinel.

These services maintain their own applicable privacy practices and policies.

12. International Data Processing

Information may be processed or stored in jurisdictions outside a user's country of residence, depending on the infrastructure and third-party services used.

Applicable data protection requirements will be considered when configuring and operating the service.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in Sentinel's features, data processing practices, applicable laws, or third-party services.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
