1. Introduction

Welcome to Sentinel, an AI-powered Discord moderation assistant designed to help authorized server moderators identify potential rule violations and maintain safe, respectful online communities.

By installing, configuring, or operating Sentinel, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, you should discontinue use of the service.

2. Purpose and Functionality

Sentinel provides automated moderation assistance through features that may include:

  • Monitoring messages in authorized Discord channels.
  • Analyzing conversations involving multiple users.
  • Identifying potential violations of server rules.
  • Detecting possible harassment, discrimination, spam, and inappropriate discussions.
  • Reviewing accessible historical messages when requested by authorized staff.
  • Generating private moderation reports.
  • Providing contextual explanations and links to relevant Discord messages.

Sentinel is designed to assist human moderators. It does not independently determine whether a Discord user has violated server rules.

3. Authorized Use

Sentinel may only be installed and operated in Discord servers with authorization from the server owner or an appropriately authorized administrator.

Operators agree to:

  • Comply with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

  • Avoid unauthorized surveillance, harassment, stalking, or discrimination.
  • Restrict access to moderation reports to authorized personnel.
  • Provide appropriate notice of monitoring where required.

4. Message Monitoring and AI Analysis

Sentinel may analyze Discord messages and surrounding conversation context to identify possible violations of server rules.

Analysis may involve message content, usernames, user identifiers, timestamps, channel information, and relevant conversation history.

Sentinel may use third-party artificial intelligence services to process relevant message information.

AI-generated assessments are not guaranteed to be accurate. Humor, sarcasm, missing context, and cultural differences may result in incorrect or incomplete assessments.

All moderation reports should be independently reviewed by authorized human moderators before disciplinary action is taken.

5. Privacy and Data Handling

Sentinel is intended to access only information available through Discord's authorized API and the permissions granted to the application.

Information may be processed for:

  • Detecting potential rule violations.
  • Generating moderation alerts.
  • Reviewing relevant conversations.
  • Preventing duplicate reports.
  • Maintaining operational settings.
  • Supporting legitimate moderation investigations.

Sentinel does not require users to provide their Discord passwords, payment information, or private account credentials.

Additional information regarding data collection, processing, retention, and deletion is provided in the Privacy Policy.

6. Moderation Reports

Sentinel may generate reports containing message excerpts, user references, channel information, timestamps, severity assessments, and links to relevant Discord conversations.

Reports are intended for authorized moderation personnel only.

Recipients must not misuse reports to harass, publicly shame, discriminate against, or unfairly target individuals.
